# Autumn Nails privacy notice draft

**Last updated:** 1 September 2026

This is a launch draft for review. It should be checked against the final business arrangements before being published on the Autumn Nails website.

## What information we collect

When you make an online booking, we collect the information needed to arrange and manage your appointment, including your name, email address and phone number.

We may keep appointment history and booking status so we can manage your appointments, deal with cancellations or queries, and apply our returning-client pricing rules where applicable.

If you choose to receive marketing, we record your marketing preference separately.

Our online booking form does not ask you to provide the detailed medical or treatment information collected on our separate consultation form.

## Why we use your information

We use booking information to arrange and administer appointments, contact you about your booking, maintain accurate appointment records, and handle reasonable queries or disputes.

Where you have actively opted in to marketing, we use that preference for the marketing purpose described when you opt in. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time.

## Calendar information

Our salon calendar is used to manage appointments. Calendar access is restricted and the information displayed is minimised so that unnecessary personal information is not exposed.

## How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including appointment administration and any applicable legal, accounting, insurance or dispute requirements.

The final retention periods for different records will be confirmed before this notice is published. We will not keep personal information indefinitely without a reason.

## Who has access

Access to booking information is limited to people who need it to operate Autumn Nails and to the service providers required to operate the booking infrastructure.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights under UK data protection law to ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, or to object to certain processing. You can also withdraw marketing consent at any time.

To make a request, contact Autumn Nails using the contact details published on the website.

## Cookies and third parties

The final published notice will identify any cookies, analytics, payment providers, hosting providers, email/SMS providers or other third parties actually used by the live website and booking system.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, systems or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on the website with its update date.

## Before publication

Confirm the final contact details, retention periods, lawful bases, actual third-party processors, cookie/analytics use, and any payment or messaging providers used by the production website.
