# Autumn Nails — Upgrade Bucket

Ideas and optional improvements to consider after the core booking system is stable and launched.

These are **not required for the initial launch** unless we later decide otherwise.

## Customer communications

- Paid email service for booking confirmations
- Owner new-booking email notifications
- Appointment reminder emails
- Cancellation/reschedule emails
- Email delivery monitoring and sender-domain authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)

## Payments

- Deposits or online payments
- Payment status in the admin booking area
- Automated payment/refund handling

## Booking improvements

- Customer self-service cancellation/rescheduling
- More advanced availability rules
- Additional appointment/service timing configuration
- Automated waitlist
- More sophisticated returning-customer history

## Client records

- Optional digital consultation records
- Richer treatment history
- Client notes where appropriate and GDPR-compliant
- Customer data export/deletion tools

## Automation & reminders

- Automated appointment reminders
- Follow-up/aftercare messages
- Review-request workflow
- Loyalty/rebooking prompts

## Infrastructure / operations

- Automated D1 backup verification and restore testing
- Additional monitoring/alerting
- Rate limiting/bot protection if booking traffic requires it
- More extensive automated end-to-end testing

## Principle

Keep the launch version simple, self-owned and low-cost. Only promote an item out of this bucket when there is a clear business benefit and we are ready to accept any ongoing cost or complexity.
