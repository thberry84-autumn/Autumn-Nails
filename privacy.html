Autumn Nails · Privacy Notice · Booking system

Your privacy.

Autumn Nails respects your privacy and aims to collect only the information needed to provide and manage appointments.

Information we collect when you book

Our online booking system may collect your first name, surname, email address, mobile number, appointment details, booking status, the price attached to the booking and whether you have chosen to receive optional marketing communications.

Why we use it

  • To arrange, manage and administer your appointment.
  • To maintain an accurate customer and booking history.
  • To contact you about an appointment or a change affecting your booking.
  • Where you separately opt in, to send occasional Autumn Nails news, appointment releases and offers.

Our legal bases

For information needed to arrange and provide an appointment, Autumn Nails relies on processing that is necessary to enter into or perform the agreement for the requested service. Optional marketing is handled separately and is based on your choice to opt in.

Your consultation information

Your Autumn Nails consultation form is kept separately from the online booking database. It may contain information relevant to treatment safety, including health or allergy information. We do not ask for that information as part of ordinary online booking.

Sharing your information

The booking system is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure and its database services. Information is used for Autumn Nails business administration and is not sold to third parties.

How long we keep information

We keep booking information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for appointment administration, customer records and applicable business or legal requirements. We will review and securely delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access your personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request erasure, restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability. You can contact Autumn Nails to exercise a relevant right or ask a privacy question.

Contact

If you have a question about your information or this notice, please contact Autumn Nails using the contact details on the website.

This notice is intended to describe the booking system's current data practices. It should be reviewed alongside Autumn Nails' wider business records and legal obligations and updated when the system changes.
