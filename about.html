The Autumn Nails feeling

A little time, just for you.

Autumn Nails is a small home studio in Portsmouth, created around a simple idea: your appointment should feel like a little pause in a busy day.

Personal

Appointments are one-to-one, with time to talk through colour, shape and the little details that make your nails feel like yours.

Calm

A cosy, relaxed setting where the focus is on you, your nails and enjoying the time rather than rushing through it.

Thoughtful

From preparation to the finished set, each appointment is approached carefully and with attention to the details.

Never rushed

Autumn Nails is deliberately small. The aim is a considered, comfortable experience rather than fitting in as many appointments as possible.

Your style

Whether you love simple, glossy colour or want to play with nail art, treatments are shaped around what you actually like to wear.

Come as you are

You don't need to know exactly what you want before you arrive. We can choose colours and details together.

Ready when you are
