# Phase 3 — Stabilisation

This phase focuses on making the existing booking system safer and easier to use day to day without introducing paid services.

- Keep the current 2-hour appointment model.
- Keep returning-customer recognition and the 21-day infill rule.
- Make availability release reject past appointment times server-side.
- Make the admin view show appointment end times and clearer status information.
- Keep consultation and health information outside the online booking database.
